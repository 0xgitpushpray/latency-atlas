    Data from RIPE Atlas, run by the RIPE NCC: the IPv4 anchoring mesh ping to pl-krk-as204841, measurement 84162223. Each anchor sends three ICMP packets every four minutes; a city shows its fastest anchor's best packet.

    An anchor pinging Kraków travels the same loop as Kraków pinging it, so the round trip is the same either way. Light in fibre covers about 204 km per millisecond; the floor is the great-circle distance at that speed, there and back.
